Buzz City

The Strange Case of Doc Jeckyll and Mr. Hyde
The Strange Case of Doc Jeckyll and Mr. Hyde — Preliminary and Alternative Pages
Rip and Wonderful Ward

Notes

The surviving material from this early comic book project is organized into a reconstructed principal Jeckyll and Hyde narrative, preliminary and alternative pages, and related adventures. The cover identifies Buzz City, No. 0, priced at 75¢ and designated Adult Humor. The group contains 27 artwork records; their inclusion does not establish that all were published together in this issue.
